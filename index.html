The trailer

“Create trailer” listens to every clip for the loudest, most sudden moments (screams, bangs, doors), then cuts them into a trailer: quiet setup shots, escalating hits with static, a fast montage under a rising shriek, a silence, the single scariest moment, and the title slamming in. Untick “In trailer” on any clip to leave it out.

The footage

Drop the videos here or click to choose them. iPhone .MOV / .MP4, portrait or landscape, HDR is fine.
Everything stays on this computer: the clips are read by your browser and never uploaded. Use a laptop or desktop, keep this tab open while it renders, and expect roughly a minute of rendering per 20 seconds of iPhone footage.
